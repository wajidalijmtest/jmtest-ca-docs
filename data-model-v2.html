1IT review fixes

2026-11-24

Revision 2026-11-24: fixes from the IT manager’s review. Everything highlighted in purple is part of this revision. It keeps the rule of the currency and country revision (no currency or country in a column name) and adds three more: a product is the same item whoever sells it, a price belongs to a price list rather than to a bare currency, and an ERP number belongs to an ERP company rather than to Business Central.
#Review pointProblem in the 2026-11-19 modelFix in this revision
1Brand per productproducts is keyed on SKU alone with one brand_id, so two brands could not sell the same SKU. Giving the second brand its own SKU was blocked too: product_erp_refs.native_number is unique per ERP, so one ERP item maps to one SKU.New catalog.brands and catalog.brand_products, keyed (brand_id, sku). slug, status, category_id, ribbon_tag and live_on_legacy_site move off products to the listing; slug becomes unique per brand.
2Price keyproduct_prices is keyed on SKU + currency, so two US storefronts could not have different USD prices, and there was no place for contract pricing. Rental and calibration prices had the same limit.New catalog.price_lists, scoped to a brand and/or country, in one currency, with a type (list, sale, contract). product_prices, product_rental_prices and calibration_prices key on price_list_id instead of currency_code.
3BC-only columnsbc_item_no on rental rates and calibration services does not work for the US, which runs on Sage 100 and CalMapp. calibration_rules had no country or brand.New catalog.erp_companies (BC-CA, SAGE100-US, CALMAPP-US). product_erp_refs keys on the company; rental terms find their item there with role rental; calibration services carry company + native number; rules gain country_code and brand_id.
4Outbox routingThe topic alone decided the target system, and the unique key (topic, aggregate_id) allowed only one system per event.destination added to commerce.integration_outbox and to its unique key: (topic, aggregate_id, destination). A US order can go to Sage 100, and one event can go to two systems with separate retries.
5valid_from on pricesvalid_from was not part of the key, so loading a future price overwrote today’s.valid_from joins the key of all three price tables. The current price is the latest row with valid_from at or before now, read through one view (catalog.current_product_prices, with matching rental and calibration views).

Also in this revision. commerce.carts, commerce.quotes and commerce.orders (not drawn) gain brand_id and country_code, which the outbox uses to pick a destination. Cart, quote and order lines snapshot price_list_id next to currency_code. product_availability is unchanged: it describes stock and lead time per country, and a brand hides a product through brand_products.status.

Migration order. Seed brands and erp_companies; apply points 3, 4 and 5 inside the jmtest.ca migration; then create price_lists and rekey the price tables (point 2); then create brand_products and move the brand-facing columns off products (point 1), before Vaetrix data lands on the shared tables.

2Currency and country revision

2026-11-19

Revision 2026-11-19: currency and country normalization. Everything highlighted in green is part of this revision. The rule it applies: no column name carries a currency (_cad, _usd) or a country (_ca, _us). Those values become rows keyed by currency_code or country_code, both backed by a reference table. Fixed per-currency price columns (list_price_usd, list_price_cad, price_cad, price_usd) are removed and shown struck through. Prices now live in rows: catalog.currencies is the reference list of supported currencies, catalog.product_prices holds one price per product per currency, catalog.product_rental_prices holds the rate and deposit of each rental tier per currency, and catalog.calibration_prices does the same for calibration services. Every money column in the model now has a currency next to it: price tables carry currency_code in their key, the cart, quote and order lines snapshot the currency with the price, and the staging extract states the currency of its prices. Adding a currency later is an insert into catalog.currencies plus price rows, with no schema migration.

Countries. catalog.countries is the reference list of markets (CA, US), each with its default currency. The country-suffixed product columns (lead_time_days_us, lead_time_days_ca, available_in_ca, rental_units_available_ca) and the country-specific switches (sale_enabled, rental_enabled, in_stock) move to catalog.product_availability, one row per product per country. The free-text region columns on rental rates and RMA requests become country_code with a reference to catalog.countries. Opening a new market is an insert into catalog.countries plus availability and price rows.
