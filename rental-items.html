Rental items: from a rental request to a rental order

An item is rentable when it belongs to a rental group in Business Central. The ERP-Sync Job copies that into the Consolidated Database (CDB). On a rental item's product page the customer always sees the rental form. Each request is saved in the CDB, the sales team gets a Klaviyo email alert, and the outbox job creates a rental Sales Quote in Business Central. The sales team then contacts the customer and converts the quote into a rental order.

1The whole flow

From the rental group to a rental order

In the background, Business Central puts items in a rental group and the ERP-Sync Job marks them as rentable in the CDB. On a rental item's product page the customer always sees the rental form, whatever the availability. When they submit it, the request and an outbox row are saved together, and Klaviyo emails the sales team. The outbox job then creates a Sales Quote in Business Central, marked as a rental. The sales team contacts the customer and converts the quote into a rental order. Nothing is paid online.

Business Central Background job Customer, apps/web, sales team Consolidated Database Notification
The alert email means “new request”, not “quote ready”.Klaviyo sends it as soon as the request is saved. The Sales Quote appears in Business Central when the outbox job runs, usually within a few minutes. If the job can't create it, a separate alert tells the team (§4).

2Rental group and rental fields

Managed in BC, synced by the ERP-Sync Job

To make an item rentable, connect it to a rental group in Business Central. On its next run the ERP-Sync Job sees the group and sets rental_enabled to true. Remove the item from the group and the job sets it back to false, and the rental form disappears from that product page. Nobody changes these fields on the storefront or in the CMS.

FieldWhat it controlsWritten by
rental_enabledTrue when the item is in a BC rental group. When true, the product page always shows the rental form.ERP-Sync Job
catalog.product_rental_ratesThe rental rates for each product, shown next to the form and used on the quote lines.ERP-Sync Job
Availability is never shown to the customer.The form doesn't check how many units are free and doesn't say whether the item is available. The sales team checks that in Business Central before they contact the customer.

3Step by step

{base} is https://api.businesscentral.dynamics.com/v2.0/{tenantId}/{environment}/api/v2.0/companies({companyId}). Every BC call sends Authorization: Bearer {access_token}. Values in the examples are illustrative.

Sync from Business Central

ERP-Sync Job, in the background

  1. E1
    BCConnect the item to a rental groupdone by a person in Business Central

    Being in a rental group is what marks an item as rentable. Its rental rates are kept in Business Central too.

    Which Business Central field holds the rental group is still to confirm with the BC admin (§6).

  2. E2
    JOBERP-Sync Job sets the rental fieldsrental_enabled · catalog.product_rental_rates

    On each run the job reads the rental group and rates from Business Central and writes them into the CDB with the rest of the catalog. Business Central always wins: a value changed in the CDB is overwritten on the next run.

    The form is shown on every rental item, every time. The page doesn't check availability and doesn't tell the customer whether units are free. Rates come from catalog.product_rental_rates.

Create the rental Sales Quote

jobs/integration-outbox, in the background

The same outbox job and the same safe-to-repeat steps as the Orders flow. The differences: the topic is rental.requested, the quote is marked as a rental, and the lines use the rental item numbers.

  1. S1
    SQLClaim pending rowscommerce.integration_outbox, topic = 'rental.requested'

    Same claim as Orders S1, with topic = 'rental.requested'. The five-minute lease stops two runs working on the same request.

  2. S2
    GETFind the customer{base}/customers?$filter=email eq 'dana@acmetesting.ca'

    An empty value array means not found: go to S2b. Because this runs on every attempt, a customer created by an earlier attempt is found here instead of being created twice.

  3. S2b
    POSTCreate the customeronly if not found{base}/customers

    Body

  "type": "Company",

  "phoneNumber": "+1 780 555 0142",
  "currencyCode": "CAD"

    Business Central fills posting groups, payment terms and tax area from the default customer template.

  4. S3
    GETCheck for an existing quote{base}/salesQuotes?$filter=externalDocumentNumber eq 'RNT-CA-000045'&$expand=salesQuoteLines

    If an earlier attempt timed out after Business Central had already created the quote, it is found here and reused. An empty value array means carry on to S4.

    The quote has to show the sales team at a glance that it is a rental. The RNT- request number in externalDocumentNumber already does this, and the sales team can search for it. A proper rental marker on the quote, such as a field added by an API extension, is an open decision (§6).

    The BC item number comes from catalog.product_erp_refs with role = 'rental', never from the SKU directly. A SKU with no rental mapping is a permanent error: the job stops and alerts instead of guessing.

4When the sync fails

Same rules as Orders

Rental requests use the same retry schedule, error handling and alerts as the Orders flow, §3: temporary errors are retried with backoff up to five attempts; permanent errors, or the fifth failure, roll back any partial quote, mark the outbox row failed, and tell the sales team. Pressing Retry in the CMS sets the row back to pending.

A failed sync never loses the request.The request is already saved in the CDB and the sales team already has the Klaviyo email, so they can contact the customer while the quote is retried or created by hand.

6Open decisions

Need an answer before build

Which BC field is the rental group?

The ERP-Sync Job needs one field to read: an item category, an attribute, or a custom field.

SuggestConfirm with the BC admin and record it next to the ERP-Sync Job mapping.

How the quote is marked as rental

The standard salesQuotes API has no rental field. The RNT- request number shows it, but it's easy to miss.

SuggestAdd a rental flag through a BC API extension, or use a separate number series for rental quotes.

Price on the quote lines

The customer sees a rate per period. The quote needs a quantity and price that cover the requested dates.

SuggestSend the rate shown and the number of periods; the sales team adjusts it on the call.

Rental form or RFQ?

Rental lines can also be quoted through the RFQ flow (Flow 1), which could create a second quote for the same request.

SuggestMake the rental form the only way to request a rental, and stop rental lines going into the RFQ cart.

Who gets the alert email

“Sales team” isn't an inbox yet.

SuggestUse the same inbox as new orders unless rentals have their own team.

Keep syncing units available?

rental_units_available_ca isn't shown to customers any more.

SuggestKeep it for the CMS so sales can see availability next to the request, or drop it from the sync.
