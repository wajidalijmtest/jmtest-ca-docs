Rental items: from Business Central to the sales team

Business Central decides what can be rented. The ERP-Sync Job copies that decision and the rental rates into the Consolidated Database (CDB). On the product page, rental items show a rental form; each request is saved in the CDB and a Klaviyo event emails the sales team.

1The whole flow

Two paths that meet in the CDB

The top path runs in the background: Business Central marks items as rentable and sets their rates, and the ERP-Sync Job writes those fields into the CDB. The bottom path starts with the customer: on a rental item's product page they fill in the rental form, the request is saved in the CDB, and a Klaviyo event sends the sales team an email alert. Nothing is paid online; the sales team follows up.

3Step by step

Sync from Business Central

ERP-Sync Job, in the background

  1. E1
    BCBusiness Central decides what is rentablerental flag · units available in Canada · rental rates

    The rental flag, the units available in Canada and the rental rates are all set on the item in Business Central. Nobody edits them on the storefront or in the CMS.

    Which Business Central fields or price lists hold these values is still to confirm with the BC admin (§5).

  2. E2
    JOBERP-Sync Job writes the rental fieldsrental_enabled · rental_units_available_ca · catalog.product_rental_rates

    On each run the job reads the rental values from Business Central and writes them into the CDB along with the rest of the catalog. Business Central always wins: a value changed in the CDB is overwritten on the next run.

    When an item stops being rentable in Business Central, the job sets rental_enabled to false and the rental form disappears from that product page.

Rental request

apps/web, while the customer waits

  1. R1
    APPShow the rental form on the product pageonly when rental_enabled = true

    The product page reads the rental fields from the CDB. Rental items show the rental form and their rates from catalog.product_rental_rates; other products don't show it.

    Rental items can't be bought at checkout. Rental lines in the cart go through the RFQ flow instead (Flow 1), and checkout refuses them.

  2. R2
    POSTSave the rental request in the CDBrental request record table name proposed

    The customer submits the form and the request is saved in the CDB first. Once it is saved, the customer sees a confirmation that the sales team will be in touch.

    The request keeps a copy of the product, the quantity and the rate shown to the customer, so a later rate change in Business Central doesn't change what the customer was quoted.

  3. R3
    APPSend the Klaviyo rental item eventafter the request is saved

    The event is sent only once the request is saved. If Klaviyo is unavailable, the request is still in the CDB, so no lead is lost; how the event is retried is an open decision (§5).

  4. R4
    EMAILKlaviyo emails the sales teamemail alert

    The email tells the sales team which item was requested, by whom, for which dates, and how to reach the customer. The sales team follows up directly with the customer.

5Open decisions

Need an answer before build

Where the values live in BC

The flag, units available and rates have to come from specific Business Central fields or price lists for the ERP-Sync Job to read them.

SuggestConfirm the fields with the BC admin and record them next to the ERP-Sync Job mapping.

No units available

When rental_units_available_ca is 0, the page could hide the form, show it with a waitlist message, or show it as normal.

SuggestKeep the form and say units are limited. Sales can still offer dates or an alternative.

Rental form or RFQ?

Rental lines can also be quoted through the RFQ flow (Flow 1). Two routes for the same request could reach the sales team twice.

SuggestDecide whether the rental form replaces the RFQ path for rentals, or creates an RFQ behind the scenes.

If Klaviyo fails

The request is saved, but the sales team won't hear about it unless the event is sent again.

SuggestSend the event through the outbox, as orders do, so it is retried and alerts after repeated failures.

Form fields

The example in R2 is a proposal. Sales should confirm what they need to quote a rental.

SuggestProduct, quantity, dates, contact details and a message, with dates optional.

Who gets the email

The diagram says “Sales team” without naming an inbox.

SuggestUse the same inbox as new orders unless rentals have their own team.
